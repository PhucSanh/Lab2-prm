Design ONE mobile screen: "ST-Loading" (saving state) for the Android app "An Nhiên" (360x800 dp).

CONTEXT: App "An Nhiên" – daily routine calendar based on the 9 Power 9 habits for Vietnamese people aged 60+. User: Bà Lan, 68, farsighted, shaky fingers, only knows Zalo/YouTube. Principles: Bác decides; no pressure; few words. All UI text in Vietnamese with diacritics, addressing the user as "Bác". Sentences max 15 words.

PURPOSE: A full-screen, calm "saving" screen shown for a moment after Bác taps "Lưu việc này". It tells Bác the app is saving and that nothing needs to be tapped. The screen closes by itself when saving finishes.

LAYOUT (360x800, screen margin 20px, background #F6F7F2, no bottom navigation, no app bar, no buttons, nothing tappable):
1. Everything is one centered column (horizontally and vertically centered in the screen), center-aligned text.
2. A circular progress ring: Lucide `loader-circle` icon 88x88px, stroke 2.5, color #1F684B, inside a 160x160px circle filled #D6EADF.
3. 32px below the circle, the text "Đang lưu việc…" in Be Vietnam Pro 30px bold (700), color #202B25.
4. 12px below, the text "Không cần bấm gì thêm" in 24px regular (400), color #526057, one line.
5. Nothing else on the screen: no buttons, no cancel link, no percentage, no progress bar, no countdown, no illustration, no card.

STYLE TOKENS: background #F6F7F2; text #202B25; secondary text #526057; primary #1F684B; container #D6EADF; font Be Vietnam Pro; every text at least 20px; contrast of text at least 4.5:1; flat design, no gradient, no shadow, no dark background, no neon.

ICONS: Use only the Lucide icon set (outline, stroke 2-2.5). Never draw custom icons.
Every icon renders at least 32x32px and its glyph fills the box.
Use exactly these icons and never reuse one icon for another meaning:
loader-circle = saving in progress (only for the "Đang lưu việc…" state)

CONSTRAINTS (from rule.md, error level):
- R-TXT-01: every text at least 20px.
- R-TXT-03: no sentence longer than 15 words; no paragraph longer than 2 lines.
- R-CON-01: text contrast at least 4.5:1.
- R-LAY-01: nothing overflows or is clipped at 360px width; no horizontal scroll.
- R-ICON-01 / R-ICON-02: every icon is accompanied by visible text (the ring is next to "Đang lưu việc…").
- R-GES-01: no swipe, drag or long-press interactions.
- R-LANG-01: 100% Vietnamese with diacritics; no English words.
- R-LANG-03 / R-VOC-01: no pressure words, no technical words (no "đồng bộ", "sao lưu", "thiết bị", "tải").
- R-CEN-01: content standing alone is centered.
Do NOT generate: leaderboard, streak, health score, charts, three-dot menu, icon-only buttons, carousel, English text, gradient, background image behind text.
